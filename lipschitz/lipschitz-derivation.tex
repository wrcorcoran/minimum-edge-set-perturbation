\section{Converting To Simplified Form}
First, our goal is to get to the Lipschitz constant form:
$$
\frac{||X^{(L)} - X^{(L)}_p||_2}{||\Delta - \Delta_p||_2} \leq L_2
$$
where $L_2$ is the Lipschitz constant. 
\\\\
From \textit{An Optimization Based Framework...} we are given:
$$
||X^{(L)} - X^{(L)}_p||_F \leq \sqrt{d} L ||\Delta - \Delta_p||_2 \prod_{l=1}^{L} ||\theta^{(l)}||_2
$$
Here are the following definitions:
\\$\Delta = $ the normalized adjacency matrix
\\$X^{(l)} = $ the embeddings at the $l$th layer
\\$d = $ dimensionality of input graph
\\$L = $ the number of layers in the GCN model
\\$p$ indicates \textit{perturbation}
\\\\
We know for a general matrix $PQ$:
$$
||PQ||_F \leq ||P||_F ||Q||_2
$$
We can use this to our advantage by doing:
$$
||I_d (X^{(L)} - X^{(L)}_p)||_F \leq ||I_d||_F ||X^{(L)} - X^{(L)}_p||_2
$$
We know that $||I_d||_F = \sqrt{d}$.
\\\\
Therefore, we can rewrite this as (\emph{Note: I'm a little shaky about this subsitution because of the} $\leq$):
$$
\sqrt{d}||X^{(L)} - X^{(L)}_p||_2 \leq \sqrt{d} L ||\Delta - \Delta_p||_2 \prod_{l=1}^{L} ||\theta^{(l)}||_2
$$
We can simplify to get:
$$
\frac{||X^{(L)} - X^{(L)}_p||_2}{||\Delta - \Delta_p||_2} \leq L \prod_{l=1}^{L} ||\theta^{(l)}||_2
$$
This was the initial form that we wanted. 
Therefore, we know that
$$
L_2 = L \prod_{l=1}^{L} ||\theta^{(l)}||_2
$$
